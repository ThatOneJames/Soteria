Insurance FAQs

Everything you need to know before buying a policy.

What does a comprehensive car insurance cover?

It usually covers accident damage, theft, total loss, third-party liabilities, and selected add-ons like roadside assistance and windshield repairs.

How do I know if my vehicle is eligible for the best rates?

Insurers typically assess the vehicle age, model, variant, usage, and location before pricing your policy.

What are repair shop networks?

These are accredited garages where you can process claims faster, often with cashless repairs and direct billing.
